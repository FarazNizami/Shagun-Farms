OUR STORY

Welcome to Shagun Farms

Established with a commitment to redefine event hospitality in Raipur, Shagun Farms has emerged as the city's preferred venue for weddings, corporate galas, and milestones. Situated strategically along VIP Road near Raipur Airport, our property bridges convenient accessibility with peaceful surroundings.

"We believe every event deserves meticulous care, blending comfort, hospitality, and unforgettable memories."

Featuring 110 elegantly appointed rooms, 4 versatile banquet halls, and vast outdoor lawns accommodating up to 6,500 guests, Shagun Farms provides complete event support for your celebrations.
